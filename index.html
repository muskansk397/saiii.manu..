Happy Birthday ra ❤️

First of all, Happiest Birthday to you! 🎂

Nee birthday ki just "Happy Birthday" ani oka normal message petti aapesthe saripodu anipinchindi. Anduke konchem naa heart lo unna things anni ila letter lo rayali anipinchindi. Direct ga cheppaleni konni feelings ni at least ila aina cheppali anipinchindi.

Actually mana friendship gurinchi alochisthe, manam eppudu ila close ayyamo naake teliyadu. Oka particular day or one big moment valla manam close avvaledu. Ala small-small conversations, random talks, jokes, teasing, laughs, silly things, misunderstandings, arguments, and chala memories… ila teliyakundane mana friendship chala special aipoyindi.

Sometimes I feel, konni people mana life loki sudden ga vastaru. Appudu vallaki mana life lo entha important place vastundo manaki kuda teliyadu. But time pass ayye koddi, vallatho create ayina small memories anni kalisi vallani chala special person ga marchestayi.

Nuvvu kuda naaku alanti person.

Nuvvu naa life lo just oka friend ani cheppadam konchem takkuve anipisthundi. Because nee place naaku chala special. Nitho matladina simple conversations kuda later gurthu vachinappudu smile vachesthundi. Mana silly talks aina, jokes aina, random discussions aina… avi appudu normal moments laga anipinchina, ippudu avi naaku chala precious memories.

Mana madhya every time everything perfect ga undadu. Sometimes misunderstandings untayi, sometimes arguments untayi, sometimes manam matladukokunda untam. Kani somehow aa things mana friendship ni break cheyyalekapoyayi. Maybe that's one of the things I really like about our bond.

Nitho comfortable ga undadam naaku chala important. Edaina random topic aina, serious matter aina, silly matter aina… nitho share cheyyagalanu ane feeling itself is special.

And honestly, nenu ekkuva express cheyyakapovachu. Konni feelings words lo cheppadam naaku easy kaadu. Sometimes normal ga behave chestha, but inside I care a lot. Nee happiness naaku genuinely important.

Nuvvu happy ga unte naaku kuda happy ga anipisthundi. Nuvvu life lo baaga achieve cheyyali, nee dreams anni fulfill avvali, and nuvvu peaceful ga undali ani genuinely wish chestha.

And there is one more thing I don't usually say.

Nenu ninnu daily miss avutha.

Sometimes every small moment lo kuda "nuvvu unte bagundhu" ani anipisthundi. Edaina interesting thing jarigina, edaina funny thing chusina, or just random ga unna… konni times automatically "nuvvu ikkada unte bagundhu" ani anipisthundi.

Distance valla manam anukunnappudu kalavalem. Everyday matladukovadam kuda possible kaadu. Kani distance unna kuda, nee presence naa mind lo chala times untundi.

Nuvvu pakkana unte simple moments kuda special ga anipisthayi.

And mana memories lo oka memory matram nenu life long marchiponu.

Train daggara nannu "Manu… Manu… Manu…" ani pilichina moment. 🥹❤️

Adi chala small moment laga anipinchachu. Maybe neeku adi just normal moment ayyi undochu. Kani naaku adi one of the most beautiful memories.

Aa moment enduku antha special ante naaku exact ga reason kuda teliyadu. But aa scene, aa moment, nuvvu ala pilichina way… anni naa mind lo ala fix aipoyayi.

Life lo enni years pass aina, aa memory gurthu vachina every time I think I will smile.

Maybe mana life lo thousands of memories create avuthayi. Chala people ni meet avutham, chala places ki veltham, chala new experiences vastayi. Kani konni moments matram heart lo permanent ga undipothayi.

Aa train daggara moment naaku alantide.

Maybe that's why small memories are sometimes more valuable than big moments.

If I Had To Explain What You Mean To Me…

Dear You,

There's something really rare about finding a person who doesn't just stay in your life — but quietly becomes a part of it. Who doesn't need big efforts or constant talking to still feel so close.

I don't say this enough. I don't really know how to say this enough. But there are some people in life you don't just call "friends." They become something bigger. Something deeper. Something that calling them a best friend feels like an understatement.

You are that someone to me.

You are the person I think about when something funny happens. You are the person I hope is proud when something good happens to me. You are the comfort in the chaos, the silence that never feels awkward, the laughter that starts from the silliest little thing.

We may not talk every day. We may not meet whenever we want. We may be far. But nothing about that changes how valuable you are. Nothing about that changes how much your presence in my life has changed me for the better.

You taught me that some people are not just passing by. They stay. They stay in your habits, in your memories, in the way you smile when you remember that one thing you both once laughed at.

Thank you for being one of those people for me.

Always,
Your friend ❤️
